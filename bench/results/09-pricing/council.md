**Launch with a $49 one-time license.** Hold off on the $29 team plan and usage-based pricing.

**Why $49:** The 12 people who asked to pay are ready to buy, so give them one price and a real checkout. A CLI tool used project by project probably suits a single purchase. Even if all 12 paid $8/month, that is only $96 a month, so a monthly plan teaches you little at this scale. Treat $49 as a guess to test.

**What the $49 covers:**
- **If the tool processes recordings on your machine or the user's,** sell $49 for the current major version, with updates until the next one.
- **If you host processing** (cloud transcription or LLM calls), set a hard monthly limit, for example 20 recordings. Sell extra packs above it. Unlimited plans don't cap your costs. Before launch, measure what one recording costs you to process.

**What to test first, in order:**

1. **Sell to the 12 this week.** Send each a Stripe link at $49, open for 7 days. Ask what they last documented, how often they do this, and who pays for their tools.
   - 4 or more purchases means go.
   - 3 or fewer means interview the people who didn't buy before you change the price.
   - With only 12 people, this is a go/no-go check, not a reliable conversion rate.
2. **Track how buyers use it** over the next 4 to 6 weeks: finished docs, use across separate weeks, and processing cost per user. If many use it every week, a subscription fits better. Offer new users $8/month or $80/year.
3. **Compare $49 against $8/month** only if step 2 points to steady weekly use. Show the two options to different groups of new users. Judge each by projected 12-month revenue after cancellations, not by cash in the first two weeks, which favors the upfront payment. With this few users, act only on large differences.
4. **Add the $29 team plan when buyers ask for it.** The signals are two buyers from the same company, a request to share output, or someone asking for a company invoice.

**Main risk:** you don't yet know how often people use it or what each recording costs you to process. Either could change the right model.
