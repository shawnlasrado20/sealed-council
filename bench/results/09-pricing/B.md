**Launch with the $49 one-time license.** Do not lead with $8/month or usage-based.

Twelve people already asked to pay. That is a close, not a pricing workshop. A CLI buyer will write a card once. $8/month trains them to cancel after the first real docs batch and nets ~$96/year only if they stay a year — most will not. Usage-based is honest if you pay for cloud transcription/LLM, but CLI users hate surprise bills, and it slows the twelve people who just want a price.

**Caveat you must ship with $49:** if processing is hosted, cap it (example: 20 recordings or 2 hours/month). Overages or an $8/month “unlimited processing” add-on cover COGS. If it is fully local, skip the meter. $49 lifetime, charge again only on a major version.

**Do not launch $29 team yet.** Nobody has asked for seats. A team SKU on a CLI is extra billing and support for a buyer you have not met.

**Test this first, in order:**

1. **Close the twelve this week** at $49, 7-day window, one SKU. If 6+ buy, $49 is at or below willingness to pay. If 2 or fewer buy, the block is packaging or trust, not $8 vs $49.
2. **Paywall the next ~50 heavy users** (3+ recordings). Split: A) $49 lifetime (with the cap if cloud) vs B) $8/month. Run two weeks. Keep the winner on **cash collected**, not signup count. $8 will often win on count and lose on money.
3. **Watch for team pull.** If two people from the same company buy, or anyone asks to share a workspace, then test $29/month team (3–5 seats, shared output). Not before.

Ignore usage-based as the storefront. Use it only as a meter inside the license if your inference bill is real.
